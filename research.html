Questions, methods and evidence

My research interests include behavioural data analysis, machine learning evaluation and data-driven systems.

Research project · MIKE 2026 submission

Predicting Depression Through Social Media Behaviour: Integrating Behavioural Features and Hybrid Methodologies

This project studies how behavioural features from social media can be explored using supervised classification and clustering methods. The work compares SVM, KNN, ID3 and Naive Bayes, alongside K-Means and K-Medoids clustering.

Focus areas: feature analysis, model comparison, evaluation metrics and interpretation of clustering results.

Important context: this is an academic prediction study, not a clinical diagnostic tool. Reported metrics should be interpreted within the dataset and experimental setup, not as evidence of clinical reliability.

My research approach

  • Understand the data source, target variable and potential bias.
  • Compare suitable baseline models rather than relying on one result.
  • Use metrics appropriate to the task and explain their limitations.
  • Keep claims consistent with reproducible outputs and project documentation.
