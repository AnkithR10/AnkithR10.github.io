First-author research · MIKE 2026 submission

Predicting Depression Through Social Media Behaviour

Integrating behavioural features and hybrid methodologies to explore predictive patterns in social-media-related data.

SVMKNNID3Naive BayesK-MeansK-Medoids

Research question

Can behavioural features derived from social-media-related data support the study of depression-related patterns using supervised classification and unsupervised clustering?

Methods

Four classification methods were compared: SVM, KNN, ID3 and Naive Bayes. K-Means and K-Medoids were explored for clustering.

Interpretation and limitations

These figures describe the recorded experimental setup only. They should not be interpreted as evidence of clinical validity or a deployable diagnostic system. Before publishing a final academic case study, verify the final dataset description, feature definitions, evaluation split, baselines, and ethics/limitations text against the submitted manuscript.

Status: submitted to MIKE 2026 at Amrita Chennai. Do not label it accepted or published unless official confirmation supports that status.
