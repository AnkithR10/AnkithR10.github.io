## About Me

I'm an **MCA student specializing in Artificial Intelligence and Data Science at Amrita Vishwa Vidyapeetham, Mysuru**. I enjoy exploring how data can explain patterns, support decisions, and help build useful intelligent systems.

My work spans **machine learning, data analysis, clustering, natural language processing, and computer vision**. I focus on understanding the problem, preparing data carefully, evaluating models with appropriate metrics, and communicating what the results actually mean.

I have also worked as a **team lead for a 22-member team** during the Infosys Springboard Virtual Internship 6.0, contributing to an AI-powered enterprise email classification project across its development modules.

## Research

### Predicting Depression Through Social Media Behaviour: Integrating Behavioural Features and Hybrid Methodologies

My research explores how behavioural features and machine learning methods can be studied together to analyse patterns in social media behaviour.

- **Research area:** behavioural data analysis and machine learning
- **Methods explored:** SVM, KNN, ID3, Naive Bayes, K-Means, and K-Medoids
- **Conference:** submitted to MIKE 2026

The work is intended for research and analysis; it should not be interpreted as a clinical diagnosis or substitute for professional care.
